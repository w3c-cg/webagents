**HG: Follow up on the trends towards agentic environment engineering **

The following note, first mentioned during the meeting by the OmniParser V2 project, captures current trends in agentic AI and highlights an ongoing shift from prompt and context engineering to environment engineering. This shift indicates a growing focus on environment representation, i.e., designing, structuring, and maintaining the environment in which an agent perceives, reasons, and acts. This includes the agent's observations, affordances, tools, states, transition dynamics, memory, constraints, and feedback mechanisms. These representations range from structured lists of interactive elements, affordances, and signifiers; through accessibility and control graphs, finite-state and state-transition models, topological memories, semantic scene graphs, structured semantic maps, and cognitive maps; to advanced predictive world models that simulate the possible consequences of actions. The references below illustrate this direction across digital environments (web, mobile, and desktop UIs), the WoTs, and embodied physical environments. The overarching goal of this trend towards explicit environment engineering is to enable a transition from reactive to deliberative agents. Ultimately, this points toward state-aware, deliberative, and predictive agents that can query explicit representations of their environments, reason about possible future states, verify action outcomes, recover from failures, and execute reliable, long-horizon planning.

The recent surveys at the end of the list on agentic world modeling (i.e., agentic environment engineering) provide broader evidence for this emerging direction:

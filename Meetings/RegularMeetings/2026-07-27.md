Andrei CIortea: Discussion on accountability. This is relevant to the Interoperability Report. Accountability is often understood as liability. However, a new understanding of accountability is emerging, in particular as a mechanism for ongoing system improvement. An article on that topic was presented by Andrei Ciortea (co-written with Stephen Cranefield) at the EMAS workshop. [Slides available on Github]([https://github.com/w3c-cg/webagents/blob/main/Meetings/RegularMeetings/2026-07-27-CiorteaCranefield-Accountability.pdf)](https://github.com/w3c-cg/webagents/blob/main/Meetings/RegularMeetings/2026-07-27-CiorteaCranefield-Accountability.pdf)).

An accountor must be able to render accounts and learn from the accountee's feedback. 

The focus is on agents equipped with procedural knowledge (e.g., skills for language agents, plan libraries for BDI agents).

Example scenario with home heating. 

An accountable agent actively creates accounts. 

LLMs are used to analyze accounts. LLMs generate corrective actions (to solve the current issue) and preventive actions (to prevent future occurrences of the problem).

LLMs are used to revise the agent's skills (e.g., Jason plans).

The results were above expectations in demonstrating that LLM-based accountability enables self-improvement. 

A broader evaluation and extensions to the accountability mechanisms are needed.

Önder Gürcan: Another meaning of accountability in the distributed systems community is responsibility. 

Andrei Ciortea: Accountability is often understood as liability, but here we explore computational accountability. The meaning of accountability needs to be made clear in the Interoperability Report once we converge on a direction.

Stephen Cranefield: The term "accountability" is overloaded and has been used in many different domains. Often, adjectives are used in addition to "accountability" to explain the meaning used, e.g. "legal accountability".

Andrei Ciortea: The entity that is held accountable may differ depending on the concept of accountability used. When accountability is used to mean liability, software agents are often not considered accountable but the entity (e.g., human, organization) behind it.

Cristina Baroglio: In some circumstances (e.g., when the environment is not deterministic), it may be possible for guarantees of non-repetition not to be fully applicable, or the accountee may not be able to provide feedback to improve the accountor.

Andrei Ciortea: This surfaces the complexity of dealing with accountability in open and dynamic systems. What we present is preliminary ongoing work, and many problems remain open. It remains to be seen whether LLMs are reliable enough for the purpose of designing self-improving accountable agents.

Terry Paine: The purpose of using accountability for self-improving seems to be about/include repairing errors in the original agent design. Wondering how this relates to norms.

Andrei Ciortea: Using accountability is more general and considers the application of norms. I think of norms as defining a standard of behavior.

Stephen Cranefield: Norms are very important for accountability. 

Terry Paine: The proposal helps formalize mechanisms for self-improvement.

Andrei Ciortea: Accountability is not merely about assigning blame but also about improving the system. Accountability is used in human society to make humans behave better.

Cristina Baroglio: Accountability refers to some form of agreement. Not all forms of accountability relationships are related to norms.

Önder Gürcan: Governance is missing from the structure of the interoperability report.    

Andrei Ciortea: This is a fair point. The scope of the Interoperability Report remains to be defined.     

Jérémy Lemée: There could be multiple accountors and accountees.

Andrei Ciortea and Stephen Cranefield: Even though the article only considers one accountor and one accountee, the mechanism could be applied to multiple accountors. There could also be multiple accountees, typically one for each task or concern.

Cristina Baroglio: The content of accounts should be discussed in the report. The link between accountability and norms is related to the concept of obligation.

Andrei Ciortea: There might be a need for a task force on accountability.
